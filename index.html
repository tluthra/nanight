A little closer, while you work.

Small touches that make checking in feel effortless.

⌃⌥⌘N

One shortcut. A quick peek.

Show or hide Nanight from anywhere on your Mac with Control + Option + Command + N. Prefer another combination? Make it yours in Settings.

Pinch to zoom in, scroll to pan, and rotate with your trackpad. Take a closer look or adjust the angle without moving your camera.
